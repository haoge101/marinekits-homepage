    Why it works

    Made on the bridge, for the bridge

    📡

    Offline at sea

    Activate once online. ETA, routes, compass error and port distances then run on your device with no signal. Only typhoon data and Notices to Mariners need a connection.

    🌀

    Typhoon from JMA

    Pulls tracks straight from the Japan Meteorological Agency and keeps the latest copy, so you can still look at it when the signal drops.

    🧭

    Built-in magnetic model

    Compass error uses the WMM2025 model built into the app, and warns you when the model is out of date.

    🗺️

    Routes you already have

    Import an RTZ file or paste waypoints copied from your ECDIS, and get the ETA for the whole passage.

    🌗

    Dark or light, EN or 中文

    A night-friendly dark theme for the bridge, a light one for the office, and one-tap language switching.

    💻

    Desktop and phone

    A Windows installer and an Android app, with the same tools on both.
